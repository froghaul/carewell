Questions to ask before a parent leaves hospital

Discharge day often comes suddenly, and the information comes fast. Most problems in the first week home come from something that wasn't asked or wasn't written down. Take this list with you.

About medicines

  • Which medicines have changed — and why? Write each one down: new, stopped, or a different dose or time.
  • How long does the hospital supply last? New medicines often come home with only a few days' worth. Get the script filled early.
  • What should we do with the old packets at home? Stopped medicines are best returned to the pharmacy so nobody takes them by mistake.

About what happens next

  • Who books the follow-up appointments and tests — the hospital, the GP, or us?
  • Can we have a copy of the discharge summary? One for you; one should go to the GP.
  • Is any help or equipment being arranged at home? Physio, nurse visits, a shower chair, rails.

About staying well

  • What should we watch for, and who do we call? Get a phone number for questions after hours.
  • Any limits? Driving, showering, lifting, eating or drinking.

The first week home

Book a GP visit within about a week, give the pharmacy the new medicine list, update any blister pack, and update the fridge emergency page.

This is general organising advice, not medical advice. Always follow the hospital team's instructions.
