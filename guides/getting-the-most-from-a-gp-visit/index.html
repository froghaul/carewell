Getting the most out of a GP visit with Mum or Dad

A standard appointment is short, and it's easy to come home realising the thing that mattered most never came up. A little preparation makes a big difference.

Before you go

  • Write down the three most important questions, most important first.
  • Note what's changed since last time — new or worse symptoms and when they started, falls or near falls, sleep, appetite, side effects, missed doses.
  • Bring the current medicine list, including anything from the chemist or health food shop.
  • Check which scripts need renewing, so it can be done in the same visit.

In the room

  • Start with the most important question — don't save it for the end.
  • If your parent agrees, it's fine to say "I help Mum with her medicines; can I ask a couple of things?"
  • Ask the doctor to write down any change to a medicine.

Straight after

Write down what was said, any changes, and what happens next — before you're back in the car. A short daily log between visits means you can answer "since when?" with a date, not a guess.

This is general organising advice, not medical advice.
