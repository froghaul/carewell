Questions

Do I need Excel?

No. It works in free Google Sheets (open it in Drive and choose "Make a copy"), and in Excel 2019 or later.

Can my brother or sister use it too?

Yes — that's the point. Share your Google Sheets copy with the family and everyone sees the same, up-to-date list.

Is my parent's information sent anywhere?

No. The file lives in your own Google Drive or computer. We never see it.

Is this medical advice?

No. It's an organiser. Always follow your doctor's and pharmacist's instructions.
